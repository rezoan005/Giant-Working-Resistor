# ⚡ Giant Working Resistor – 50× Scale

**An oversized, functional replica of a standard electronic resistor, built approximately 50 times larger than its original counterpart.**

Ever wondered what a resistor would look like if it were 50 times bigger? I decided to turn that idea into a real engineering project!

This project features a giant, functional resistor designed and fabricated to demonstrate the concept of electrical resistance through an oversized physical model.

The design files are shared openly so makers, students, engineers, and electronics enthusiasts can recreate, modify, and experiment with the project.

## 🚀 Project Highlights

- **50× Scale:** An oversized physical representation of a standard resistor.
- **Functional Prototype:** Built to operate as a working resistor.
- **3D Printable Design:** STL files provided for fabrication.
- **Open Source:** Design files available for the maker community.
- **Educational Purpose:** Makes an everyday electronic component more tangible and engaging.

## 🛠️ Fabrication

The project is designed around 3D-printable components.

1. Download the STL files from the `STL` directory.
2. Import the models into your preferred slicer.
3. Configure your printing parameters according to your printer and material.
4. Print the individual components.
5. Assemble the components and complete the electrical connections according to the design.

**Note:** Printing parameters, material selection, and electrical connections may require adjustments depending on your fabrication setup.

## 🖨️ Required Tools

- FDM 3D Printer
- 3D Printing Slicer (Cura, PrusaSlicer, etc.)
- Basic Electronics Tools
- Appropriate electrical connection components

## ⚠️ Safety Disclaimer

This is an experimental educational project. Always verify the resistance, power rating, temperature, and electrical characteristics before connecting the device to a circuit.

Do not exceed the safe operating limits of the materials or components used. Exercise appropriate electrical safety precautions during testing.
